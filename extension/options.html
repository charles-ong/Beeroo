Beeroo Price Contributor

Help people compare beer prices across Dan Murphy's, BWS and Liquorland. This extension is off until you turn it on below.

What it does

  • While you browse beer pages on those three sites logged out, it reads the product list your browser already received and sends a small, filtered copy to Beeroo.
  • It never makes requests of its own, clicks, scrolls, or loads pages for you.
  • It sends product names, sizes, ABV, pack sizes and prices, plus the store's name, state and postcode. Not your name, address, cookies, cart, wish list or login.
  • If it can't tell you're logged out, it sends nothing.
  • Your install ID is a random code (not linked to you). Beeroo stores only a salted hash of it.
