Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. A plain-English starting point; have it reviewed by a lawyer, especially once you collect email addresses at scale.

What we collect

  • Browsing the site: your state choice and your answer to the "18 or over" question are saved in your browser (localStorage) so you aren't asked every time. They aren't sent to us.
  • Price alerts: if you create one we collect your email address, the beer, pack size, state and target price you chose. If you used the form before, the email is also remembered in your browser to save typing.
  • Hosting logs: our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.

We don't run advertising trackers or sell personal information.

How we use it

Your email is used only to confirm and send the price alerts you asked for, and to answer you if you contact us. We'll only email you about marketing if you separately opt in.

Who handles it

We use service providers to run the site and send email: Cloudflare (hosting and alert storage) and Resend (email delivery). They may process data outside Australia, including in the United States. We don't share your email with anyone else, except where the law requires.

Keeping and deleting it

Every alert email contains an unsubscribe link which deletes that alert. If you want all your data removed, email us and we'll delete it. Unconfirmed alerts are discarded automatically.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, tell us first; you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
